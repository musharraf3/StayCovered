Citations — machine-verified

[DL-RECON] The 90-day reconsideration window verified
“If coverage is terminated for a procedural reason — missing paperwork, an unanswered notice — there is a 90-day reconsideration period after the termination date. Returning the renewal information within it lets the state reopen the case without a brand-new application, and coverage is restored back to the termination date if eligibility is confirmed. Many people never learn this window exists.”
[RT-HELP] Free human help exists verified
“Certified navigators, community health workers, legal-aid offices, and the state Medicaid ombudsman help with renewals, exemptions, and hearings at no charge. For a passed deadline, a denied exemption, conflicting records, or any notice that does not make sense, a navigator or legal aid should be involved — these are exactly the cases they exist for.”
