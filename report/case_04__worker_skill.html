[DOC-WORK] Proving work hours verified
“Pay stubs covering the month are the cleanest proof of employment hours. If pay stubs are unavailable — cash pay, a small employer, lost records — an employer letter stating hours works, and where records genuinely cannot be produced, a self-attestation with the employer's contact information is accepted while the state verifies.
