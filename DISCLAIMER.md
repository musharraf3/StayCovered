# Disclaimer

**StayCovered is a personal demonstration project.** It is not a product, not
legal advice, and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or based on any employer's
data, processes, or systems — nor with any government agency.

- **All data is synthetic.** Every member, notice, case, and situation in this
  repository was invented for this project. "Demo State" is fictional.
- **Rule summaries are simplified.** The plain-language summaries in `data/`
  reflect the general shape of the 2025 federal community-engagement
  requirements, exemption categories, and procedural windows, written for this
  project. They are illustrative, not authoritative, and states vary. They
  must not be relied on for real eligibility, renewal, or legal decisions.
- **StayCovered files nothing and decides nothing.** It demonstrates an
  architecture in which software prepares a person — screening, math,
  checklists, plain language — while every consequential step remains with
  the individual, their helpers, and the state agency.
- **It helps people comply with the rules**, claim exemptions they are
  legally entitled to, and meet deadlines. It contains no functionality for
  evading, misreporting, or gaming any requirement, and takes no position on
  the underlying policy.

If Medicaid renewal affects you in real life: contact your state Medicaid
agency, a certified navigator or community health worker, a legal-aid office,
or your state's Medicaid ombudsman. Their help is free.
